# Notice

## Attribution and Historical Lineage

**CiteFirst** originated as a development from the **CRAG-Pipeline** group project created at Manipal Institute of Technology by:

- **Sushant Shekhar Sinha**
- **Kanan Gupta**
- **Qusai**

The original collaborative repository is located at:
[https://github.com/Sushant-py/CRAG-Pipeline](https://github.com/Sushant-py/CRAG-Pipeline)

Subsequent design, citation-grounding architecture, corrective retrieval-augmented generation pipelines, evaluation frameworks, security hardening, and maintenance have been developed by Qusai under the **Qusai7-04/CiteFirst** project.
